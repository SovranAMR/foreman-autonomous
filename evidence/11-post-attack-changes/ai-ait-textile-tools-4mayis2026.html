What are the workflow benefits of using the 16 different tools in the Textile AI Tools ecosystem together?
The textile production process consists of multi-layered stages such as design, reporting, color separation, and presentation. Our 16 specialized tools offer algorithms tailored to each of these stages. While users create patterns with one tool, they can perform seamless pattern checking with another and conduct 3D fitting with yet another. Does this Textile AI Tools 16 tool framework limit or support designers' creativity?
Our AI tools do not replace the designer; they free the designer from repetitive and technically burdensome tasks such as reporting, cleaning, or creating variations. Designers can now spend their time on “creative direction” rather than “operational tasks.” The combination of AI’s speed and human vision opens the door to collections of unprecedented complexity and originality in the textile industry.
