Frequently Asked Question

Features you need to know to use the our Textile Pattern Products effectively

Which AI model does AIT AI Tools use?
Our AI model was developed entirely using our own proprietary models, trained with hundreds of thousands of authentic textile patterns specifically for the industry. Consequently, all our tools deliver results focused exclusively on textiles. Our model is not trained on generic internet data; instead, it is built upon a foundation of real textile pattern archives and deep manufacturing experience. With millions of specialized parameters, our tools are designed to “think” like a textile designer. This allows us to produce professional textile patterns that are not just random images, but designs that look flawless and are ready for direct-to-fabric printing.
Which industries can benefit from AIT AI solutions?
AIT AI Tools optimizes all processes from creative pattern design to digitalization in sectors such as clothing, fashion, and home textiles. While our platform performs original pattern design, perfect seamless tiling, and technical color separation in seconds, it offers an AI architecture that understands production constraints. It combines 2D and 3D draping technologies to present designs in the most realistic way without the need for physical samples. On the e-commerce side, it accelerates sales processes by instantly creating high quality sales visuals of products on models or in spaces through AI based mockup solutions.
What is the difference between AIT AI Tools and other AI tools?
While general AI tools are limited to generating basic visual outputs, AIT AI offers an end-to-end digital design ecosystem optimized specifically for the technical requirements of the textile industry. It ensures a professional workflow that maintains collection integrity through Pantone color-sensitive prompt management, similar pattern generation, and moodboard integration.
